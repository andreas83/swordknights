; The Aqueduct
;
; Chapter III of the Adventure. A DRAFT: docs/campaign.md is the design, this
; file is its level, 128 x 28. Sunset -- the sun on the skyline, every shadow
; at its longest. The masons' ruined quarter, then the aqueduct that crosses
; the ravine to the Mirror Tower. The stone here is tired: it gives under
; weight.
;
; @time sunset
; (Directives are proposed; the foundation decides the final syntax.)
;
; Legend, existing (scripts/world/tiles.gd):
;   #  solid        =  one-way platform    H  ladder       :  backdrop
;   ^  spikes       ~  water               @  spawn        *  relic
;   D  exit door    t  torch               p  sentinel
;   %  false wall   &  cracked wall/floor  L  inscription
;   A  arch   |  column   (architecture marks; the rest is dressed by rule)
; Legend, proposed (docs/campaign.md). None of these parse yet -- an unknown
; glyph reads as empty air, so play and prove the _static file instead:
;   o  pressure plate: a floor stone       !  portcullis, hung from a lintel
;   1-9  mason's mark: stone carved with a channel number. It wires the one
;        mechanism it touches. Same mark, same channel.
;   [ ]  a lift's platform at its home stop    '  the lift's travel
;   ,  crumbling stone     x  sun mirror (turned by the sword; starts facing up)
;   O  sun-seal door       Q  sun-sealed secret (a third seal)
;   z  scythe on a beat    i  spike slot on a beat
;   w  Bastion   j  Caracal   g  Glassbearer   Y  owl   E  the Eclipse
;
; Beat sheet, left to right (columns):
;   1. The masons' gate (0-9). Spawn in the ruined quarter; doves on the
;      lit flags.
;   2. The first crumble (10-14). Two cracked slabs over a cellar two deep.
;      Walk on: they shudder, then let you down -- harmless; grab out. Run
;      on: they fall behind you. Secret C is the cellar's east wall.
;                                            [crumbling stone: introduce]
;   3. The walk (16-34). Grab onto a walk over a walled yard. Below it a
;      Caracal stalks the doves: crouch, pounce, skid. It cannot reach you
;      up here. R1 on the walk.              [Caracal: introduce]
;   4. The cat's yard (35-46). Drop in. A Caracal on open ground: backstep
;      the pounce, cut it in the skid.       [Caracal: develop]
;   5. Two roofs (47-59). House A, three slabs, house B. Run the slabs, or
;      fall four tiles into the lane between and climb back out -- secret
;      A is in house B's face, down there. Torch 1 on house B.
;                                            [crumbling stone: develop]
;   6. The deck (60-80). The aqueduct over the ravine: three slabs over the
;      void, and a Caracal on the stone beyond them. Wait back on the deck
;      and it stalks you across them and crouches on them -- and a cat
;      cannot push off crumbling stone.
;                                 [crumbling stone + Caracal: twist]
;   7. The way down is the floor (81-86). A ruined tower stands on the
;      deck. Stand on the two cracked slabs in front of it, and wait. R2 is
;      on the tower's top, up its face by one crumbling stone: grab it,
;      stand, and jump at once.              [crumbling stone: twist]
;   8. The channel (82-100). The covered water-channel, shafts of red light
;      through its vents, a sentinel in the low dark. Secret B is a cracked
;      floor over the wide pier: jump, then plunge.
;   9. Torch 2, at the channel's mouth (101).
;  10. The collapse (101-117). The open channel over the ravine: runs of
;      three slabs between the piers that hold. Once you are on it, it falls
;      in a chain behind you -- faster than a walk, slower than a run.
;                          [crumbling stone: master; SET PIECE]
;  11. The tower's foot (118-127). The last Caracal waits on the stone, with
;      the collapse at your back: a backstep here costs ground you no longer
;      have. The door is cut in the Mirror Tower's first course.
;                                            [Caracal: master]
;
; Secrets:
;   A. House B's west face, at the lane: a false wall. A relic and WE LEFT /
;      BY NIGHT.
;   B. The channel's floor, over the wide pier: cracked, two wide. A relic.
;   C. The first cellar's east wall: cracked. A relic -- the first thing in
;      the chapter that gave way under you was a door.
;
; Relics: R1 (the walk), R2 (the tower's top), and one in each secret: five.
; Par 2:15. Expert line, about 0:45: run every slab, cut the cats out of the
; air instead of backstepping, take the channel's door the moment you reach
; it.
;
; The rule this draft is built on: crumbling stone comes in runs of at most
; three between solid stone at least two wide. With every slab fallen, each
; crossing is still a gap_tiles() jump from a two-tile runway, so nothing
; that falls can strand anyone.
;
