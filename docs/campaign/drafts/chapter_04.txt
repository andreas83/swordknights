; Beat sheet, bottom to top (rows):
;   1. The square (40-48). Spawn. The Well's mouth is set in the paving, a
;      sun carved in it, dark. A Glassbearer paces in the shadow, his glass
;      dull: a glass only burns in the sun.  [Glassbearer: introduce]
;   2. The hall (44-47). A sentinel, and Chapter II's lift up a floor.
;      Secret A is off the room it reaches.
;   3. The face, in shade (32-40). Out onto the balconies: torch 1, a grab,
;      R1, a grab.
;   4. The first light (row 31): the horizon. A mirror on the balcony
;      throws the sun at the sky. Strike it once: it turns a quarter, east,
;      into the sun carved on door 1, which fills and opens.
;                                            [mirrors: introduce]
;   5. The stairwell (22-31). The open door lets the sun onto mirror A; A
;      throws it up to B; strike B east, into door 2. Torch 2. Secret B:
;      strike A east instead.                [mirrors: develop]
;   6. The lift shaft (17-28), up to R5.
;   7. R5 (17-19). A Glassbearer on the balcony outside fires through the
;      window. Parry his bolt home. Torch 3.  [Glassbearer: develop]
;   8. R6 (9-15). No sun reaches in. The Glassbearer on the balcony
;      outside fires through the doorway at anything in line -- and mirror
;      C stands in the line. Stand behind C and let him shoot: C throws his
;      bolts up to D; strike D east, into door 3. Kill him instead and
;      mirror E, outside in the sun, does the same work. R2 is on a pillar
;      three tiles out from his balcony.     [mirrors + Glassbearer: twist]
;   9. The stair and the roof hatch. Torch 4 on the roof.
;  10. The summit (row 6). A Glassbearer in the last gold, a sentinel at
;      the parapet, and the great mirror hanging off its end, throwing the
;      sun at the sky. Strike it twice: east, then down. The beam falls the
;      tower's whole height into the Well's mouth; the mouth carries mark 9
;      and so does the great door, which opens as the sun goes down.
;                      [mirrors + Glassbearer: master; SET PIECE]
;
; Secrets:
;   A. The room the lift reaches: its east wall is cracked. A relic and
;      NO KING / KEEPS IT.
;   B. The stairwell's east wall: a doorway with a sun carved in it (Q).
;      Turn mirror A east -- away from B -- and it opens. A relic.
;   C. The hall's floor: cracked, two wide. A relic.

; Relics: R1 (balcony E4), R2 (the pillar), and one in each secret: five.

; letting the Glassbearer outside do the work.
