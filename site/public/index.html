What works where

  • Adventure, Endless and Feel Lab play with a keyboard, a gamepad or touch. On a touchscreen the left half is a floating stick, and JUMP and SWORD sit on the right.
  • Duel, the tug of war, is two players sharing one keyboard. It has no touch controls yet, so on a phone or tablet it cannot be played.
  • Duel Online is still the earlier, round-based duel, and needs a relay server you run yourself (see the netplay notes). None stands behind this page.
  • Sound starts with your first key press or tap: browsers keep a page silent until then.

Installing it

  1. Open this page on the phone and tap Download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks whether to allow installing apps from your browser, or from your file manager; allow it for that one app.
  3. Tap Install. Android may warn that the app is from an unknown source; that is what any APK from outside a store looks like.

Uninstall any earlier copy first. Every build is signed with a fresh debug key, and Android refuses to install an app over one signed differently — so a new debug build will not install over an older one, nor over a release build of the same game, and the other way round. Uninstalling clears the game's saved settings.

The game

The adventure

A platformer with the cadence of Prince of Persia: movement with weight to it, jumps you commit to, and ledge grabs that happen by themselves when you fall past a corner while moving into it. Press up to mantle, down to let go.

Adventure is the hand-built city, starting on the Sun Terrace. Endless chains generated levels, and each one is proven reachable before you are given it. Feel Lab lines up every kind of obstacle side by side, to try the movement on.

The duel

A tug of war in the spirit of Nidhogg: one hit kills, and every kill sends the world scrolling toward the killer's gate. Hold the sword high, middle or low; meet a thrust at its height and the blades clash, move into its height just as it starts and you disarm the attacker. A sword can be thrown, and an empty-handed fighter still has a sweep and a stamp.

It is being rebuilt on that model now, so expect it to change.

The look

It is late afternoon in a stone city and the sun is nearly on the horizon. Every surface is either lit gold or lost in crimson shadow, and the line between them is hard.

There are no hand-drawn assets. Terrain, skyline, characters, lettering, effects and sound are all generated by code from one small palette and one tuning table — including the images on this page, which are rendered from the game itself.

Holding neither up nor down is the middle stance. Up + throw angles the throw upward. An empty-handed fighter sweeps on the ground and stamps from the air, and picks a sword up by holding down over it. The bindings are keyboard-only by design: a Godot action cannot tell two gamepads apart.
