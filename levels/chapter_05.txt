; The Sun Well
;
; Chapter V of The Last Light (docs/campaign.md, "V. The Sun Well"). Night.
; The last light the tower sent down is kept in the Well under the city, and
; the King has gone down after it. Underground the only light is fire. The
; Well's machinery keeps time: 72 beats a minute, which at 60 Hz is exactly
; 50 frames a beat, so every blade here cuts on the beat or the off-beat.
;
;! time night
;! channel 4 beat 0
;! channel 5 beat 50
;! checkpoints route
;
; Legend (scripts/world/tiles.gd): # solid, : backdrop, H ladder, @ spawn,
; * relic, t torch, p sentinel, & cracked, % false wall, L inscription,
; | column, D the Vault's door. 4 and 5 are mason's marks: stone carved
; with the beat channel of the one blade each touches. z scythe (Scythe),
; i spike slot (SpikeSlot), Y owl (Owl), [ ] the lift's slab at home and '
; its travel (Lift).
;
; Beat sheet, from the top left down to the bottom right:
;   1. The night square. The Well's mouth stands open, and on the flags
;      before it, where the doves pecked by day, an owl, hooting softly.
;   2. The mouth's ladder. On the rungs you are off the stone and the owl
;      spreads its wings; you are under the square's floor, out of its
;      sight, before it stoops, and it folds.     [owl: introduce]
;   3. The rim gallery. Torch 1. A scythe keeps time in a doorway: a
;      pendulum parked high, then ratchet, cut, park on the other side. Go
;      through while the doorway is empty. R1 past it. Secret A by the
;      ladder.                                     [blades: introduce]
;   4. Five tiles down, the spike corridor: slots off the beat, slots on
;      it, a scythe off it. Run the rhythm.        [blades: develop]
;   5. The sump. Five down, under an owl in a niche in the west wall, the
;      way you came down. Out by a hop to the shelf and a hang to the rim:
;      a decisive climber is up before it can stoop, or climb while it pulls
;      up, or jump and slash it. R2 on a lone stone over the sump, a jump
;      from the corridor or a grab from the shelf, in the owl's sight. Secret
;      B in the wall under the owl. Torch 2 on the rim.  [owl: develop]
;   6. The throat: a ten-second lift ride down twenty-six courses, a
;      scythe every four from alternate walls on alternate beats. The slab
;      is two cells wide and each scythe sweeps one half: stand on the other
;      half, and step across in time -- left, right, left.
;                                      [blades: twist; SET PIECE]
;   7. Torch 3, on the landing at the throat's foot, where the ride comes
;      out through a doorway the hall cannot see into. Secret C in the
;      floor beside it.
;   8. The antechamber. A sentinel's hall, a step up from the landing,
;      under an owl on a column: fight him on your feet, and hop short. Off
;      the hall's end, three tiles to the scythe's door: there, jump half a
;      height between the cut and the off-beat, step back, and the owl's
;      dive reaches into the door as the blade comes through on the beat --
;      the machine kills it. Then two slots under a second owl watching from
;      the far wall, which takes anything that jumps: run them on the beat
;      instead of hopping them.  [owl: twist and master; blades: master]
;   9. The Vault's door. Beyond it, the Eclipse: the campaign's finale, its
;      own entry and its own level.
;
; Relics: R1 (the rim gallery), R2 (the lone stone over the sump), and one
; in each secret: five. Par 2:30 for this chapter; the bible's 4:00 is with
; the Eclipse.
;
