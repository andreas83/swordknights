; The West Gate
;
; Zone 1 of 5. Player one's endpoint: the arch at the far left, its mouth in
; shadow and the crowd beyond. Reaching it with the initiative wins the match.
;
; Legend (see scripts/world/tiles.gd):
;   #  solid   =  one-way platform   H  ladder   :  background niche
;   ^  spikes  ~  water   t  torch   D  the gate (the endpoint)
;
; THE LAST STAKE. The strip gets more dangerous toward each end, the way a
; Nidhogg screen does: the middle court has no hazard at all, the cistern one
; zone out has spikes but a stone to cross on, and here, one jump from the
; gate, the moat is open water with nothing over it. A runner carrying the
; initiative has to jump it, under the dusk that falls on this room, and a
; runner who walks into it hands the rope back two screens from winning.
;
; Two tiles, not three. The duel fighter has no ledge grab, so any gap is a
; hazard, and its jump carries about 42 px at a run: over a three-tile moat
; that leaves one frame to press in, which is a coin toss, not a stake. Over
; two there is a comfortable window -- see the moat note in
; docs/duel-match.md for the measurement.
;
; The gate's lintel sits at rows 13-14, so the five rows above the floor on the
; approach are clear. That is not decoration: when the leader is pinned at the
; final zone the opponent spawns right in front of the gate -- between the moat
; and the arch -- and the leader has to be able to go over the top of them
; rather than be forced to fight.
