; The Long Arcade
;
; Chapter II of The Last Light (docs/campaign.md, "II. The Long Arcade"). The
; golden hour: the sun a hand above the roofs. The city is closing its gates,
; and the gates are counterweighted -- weight is the chapter's verb, and the
; counterweight hanging beside every gate is its clock.
;
;! time golden
;! channel 1 latch
;! channel 2 hold 4.0
;! channel 3 hold 2.2
;! channel 4 hold 1.2
;! channel 5 hold 1.7
;! channel 6 hold 1.6
;! channel 7 hold 1.6
;
; Legend (scripts/world/tiles.gd), beyond Chapter I's:
;   o  pressure plate: a floor stone      !  portcullis, hung from a lintel
;   1-9  mason's mark: stone carved with a channel number, wiring the one
;        mechanism it touches. Same mark, same gate.
;   [ ]  the lift's slab at its home stop     '  the lift's shaft
;   h  an iron grate in a floor: walk it, drop through it (down + JUMP),
;      or plunge through it (down + SWORD); the sun falls through it
;   w  Bastion: a posted guard behind a tall shield
;
; Beat sheet, left to right (columns):
;   1. The road in (0-12). Plate 1 lies across the road; its stone carries
;      mark 1, and so does gate 1's lintel. Step on it: the counterweight is
;      hauled up and stays up, and so does the gate.  [counterweights: introduce]
;   2. The lift court (13-18). The ridden lift, five courses up to the
;      rampart; R1 on the stall. An expert grabs across the shaft instead.
;                                                     [counterweights: develop]
;   3. The rampart (19-25). A sentinel walks it.
;   4. The courtyard (26-38). A Bastion is posted under the rampart's lip:
;      jump off and plunge. A shove here only puts you against the rampart,
;      where secret B is.                             [Bastion: introduce]
;   5. The race street (41-54). Plate 2 holds gate 2 for four seconds; hop
;      the stall at a run. Secret A is the cracked paving beside the stall.
;                                                     [counterweights: develop]
;   6. Torch 1 (56).
;   7. The balcony (58-61). Optional: a grab onto one course of stone, a
;      sentinel on four tiles of it, R2. The street runs on underneath, and
;      the balcony's east half is an iron grate the sun falls through: the
;      hatch shown, safely, before the corridor needs one.
;   8. The weighed gate (62-72). A Bastion's weight on plate 3 holds gate 3
;      open: leap him and walk through, or plunge him and race its 2.2 s.
;                                                     [counterweights: twist]
;   9. The corridor (78-94). Two courses high, a Bastion in it: no leap, no
;      height to plunge from. The sun falls through an iron grate in its
;      roof, three wide, onto his helm under its middle. Up onto the roof (three courses, a grab), and
;      down + SWORD on the grate: through it, point first, onto him.
;                                                     [Bastion: twist]
;  10. Torch 2 (91), at the corridor's far end.
;  11. The Long Arcade (95-119). Four plates, four gates, each hold about
;      half a second behind a prince running flat out: click, run, slam. A
;      kerb in the second bay, a three-deep pit in the third (its east wall
;      is false: secret C), and a Bastion on the last plate.
;                                   [counterweights + Bastion: master; SET PIECE]
;  12. The plaza (120-125). The door, in the gold.
;
; Secrets: A, the race street's cracked paving (SAME MARK / SAME GATE); B,
; the rampart's face, a false wall; C, the arcade pit's east wall, false.
; Relics: R1 (stall), R2 (balcony), one in each secret. Par 1:45.
;
