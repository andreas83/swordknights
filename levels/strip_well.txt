; The Clockwork Well
;! time night
;
; A duel strip (scripts/duel/duel_strips.gd): its zones, west to east, read by
; scripts/duel/zones.gd exactly as levels/duel_zones.txt is.
;
; The Well at night, lit by its torches, with the machine that keeps its time
; still running: scythes that swing across the lane at head height on the beat
; and spike slots that come up on the off-beat, all on the match's own frame
; (StripHazards). Seventy-two beats a minute -- the Well's tempo in the
; adventure -- is fifty frames a beat, so the whole strip cuts every half
; second, high and low in turn.
;
; WHAT IT DOES TO A DUEL. Nidhogg is a game of heights, and here the room
; plays one too. A scythe kills a standing fighter and passes over a ducking
; or rolling one; a slot kills anything on it and nothing beside it. So the
; stance you hold and the moment you step are both answers to the clock as
; well as to the other blade, and a fighter who reads the beat can make the
; other one stand where it is about to cut.
;
;   zone 3  the Escapement      the start: a scythe each side, slots between
;   zone 2  the Gallery of Hours  a slot field below, a scythed bridge above
;   zone 1  the Gate of Hours   slots then a scythe before the arch
;
; Zones 4 and 5 are 2 and 1 reversed, every blade on its twin's beat.
