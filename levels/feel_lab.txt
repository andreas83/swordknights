; change makes any station below unclearable, the grammar moved.

;   5  long gap               a committed running jump; 3 tiles is the limit
;   6  drop                   four tiles off the wall: a soft landing
;   7  ladder + big drop      seven tiles: a hard landing, and camera shake
;   8  one-way platform       hop up through it, press down+jump to fall through
