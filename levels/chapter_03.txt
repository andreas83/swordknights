; The Aqueduct
;! time sunset
;
; Chapter III of The Last Light, at sunset: the masons' ruined quarter, then
; the aqueduct that crosses the ravine to the Mirror Tower. The old stone is
; tired, and the King's cat hunts across it. docs/campaign.md has the design;
; this is its level, 128 x 28, built from docs/campaign/drafts/chapter_03.txt.
;
; Legend beyond scripts/world/tiles.gd's terrain:
;   ,  crumbling stone (scripts/mechanisms/crumbling_stone.gd)
;   j  Caracal (scripts/entities/caracal.gd)
;   v  doves (scripts/entities/dove_flock.gd)
;
; Beats, left to right (columns):
;   1. The masons' gate (0-10). Doves on the flags by the spawn.
;   2. The first crumble (11-12): two slabs over a cellar two deep. Walk on
;      and they let you down, harmlessly; run and they fall behind you.
;      Secret C is the cellar's east wall.          [crumbling: introduce]
;   3. The walk (16-37), grabbed onto from the gate. Under it a walled yard
;      where a Caracal hunts doves: crouch, pounce, miss, skid. It cannot
;      reach you up there. R1 on the walk.           [Caracal: introduce]
;   4. The cat's yard (35-46). Drop in off the walk's end, under which there
;      is room to backstep: backstep the pounce, cut it in the skid.
;                                                    [Caracal: develop]
;   5. Two roofs (47-59): house A, three slabs, house B. Run them, or fall
;      four tiles into the lane and climb out. Secret A is house B's face,
;      down in the lane. Torch 1 on house B.         [crumbling: develop]
;   6. The deck (60-83): three slabs (65-67) over the void and a Caracal
;      beyond them. Wait at the deck's west end (60-62): it stalks you onto
;      the slabs to crouch, and a cat cannot push off crumbling stone.
;                                                   [twist, both]
;   7. The way down is the floor (84-85): stand on the slabs before the
;      ruined tower and wait. R2 is on the tower's top, up its face by one
;      crumbling stone: grab it, stand, jump at once.  [crumbling: twist]
;   8. The channel (82-100): a sentinel in the low dark, red light through
;      the vents. Secret B is the cracked floor over the wide pier.
;   9. Torch 2 at the channel's mouth (101).
;  10. The collapse (103-115): runs of three between the piers. It falls in
;      a chain behind you; a slab gone from under you is a ledge grab to the
;      next pier.                      [crumbling: master; set piece]
;  11. The tower's foot (116-127): the last Caracal, with the collapse at
;      your back. The door is cut in the Mirror Tower's first course.
;                                                    [Caracal: master]
;
; Secrets: A, house B's face in the lane (false wall; a relic and WE LEFT /
; BY NIGHT); B, the channel's floor over the wide pier (cracked); C, the
; first cellar's east wall (cracked). Relics: R1, R2 and one per secret.
; Par 2:15.
