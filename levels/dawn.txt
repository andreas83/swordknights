; Dawn
;
; The one frame of The Last Light lit from the east (docs/campaign.md,
; "Decided by the owner"): the still after the Eclipse, under DAWN and THE
; SUN REMEMBERS. Not a level to play -- nothing here is reachable or meant
; to be -- but a level file all the same, so the frame is dressed, cast and
; shaded by exactly the renderer that draws every chapter.
;
; IT IS WRITTEN MIRRORED. The rows below are the frame as the shadow solve
; sees it, with the sun where the solve always has it, in the west; DawnFrame
; shows it reflected, which puts the sun in the east. The atlas and the
; prince are drawn lit from the west too, so reflecting the whole frame is
; what lets one solve, one atlas and one figure agree that the light comes
; from the right -- a mask mirrored on its own would light the stones from
; one side and throw their shadows from the other. Read a row right to left
; to see it as the player will.
;
; Dressed by its author alone (`;! dress authored`): the rules would put a
; colonnade and a foreground archway into a picture whose subject is one
; figure on an empty floor. The doorway in the west wall is the one he came
; up out of, the Vault's door to the dawn.
;
; No `;! time`: the reach is the afternoon's three tiles, and the grade is
; the dawn's own (DawnFrame).
;! dress authored
