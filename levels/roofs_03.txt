; The Ridge
;
; Zone 3 of 5 of the Rooftops, where every match starts: symmetric about its
; own centre, one spawn mark, mirrored by the match.
;
; Three roofs and the two alleys between them, two tiles across, with nothing
; at the bottom: a missed jump is a fall out of the city. A chimney in the
; middle of the centre roof, two tiles tall and four across, is the one thing to vault, and
; the washing on the lines either side of it hangs in front of everything.
; The fighters start each on an outer roof, so the first exchange is decided
; by who crosses an alley first -- and a clash at an alley's lip throws the
; loser over it.
;
; Static and obvious, all of it: the alleys are the strip's only hazard and
; they never move.
