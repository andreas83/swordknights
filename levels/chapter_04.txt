; The Mirror Tower
;
; Chapter IV of The Last Light, 46 x 52, at dusk. The design is
; docs/campaign.md ("IV. The Mirror Tower"); this is its level, built from
; docs/campaign/drafts/chapter_04.txt. The lower town is already in the
; city's shadow; only the tower's upper courses are still gold. Climb toward
; the light, and send the last of it down the Well.
;
;! time dusk
;! horizon 32
;! channel 9 latch
;! checkpoints route
;! mark G sun_mirror great=1 sunset=9
;
; Legend, beyond the usual (scripts/world/tiles.gd):
;   x  sun mirror: turned a quarter clockwise by the sword, starts facing up
;   G  the great mirror: a sun mirror, drawn large, and the one whose
;      wire, 9, sets the sun when it latches (the mark above)
;   O  sun-seal door: opens when light rests on it, and stays open
;   Q  sun-sealed secret: the same seal over a secret room
;   g  Glassbearer: fires sunbolts while his glass is in the sun
;   9  mason's mark: the Well's mouth and the great door share wire 9
;   [ ] '  Chapter II's lift: the slab at its home stop and its shaft. Both
;          of the tower's lifts are ridden, from the bottom up, and go home
;          when they are left (scripts/mechanisms/lift.gd).
;
; Beat sheet, bottom to top (rows):
;   1. The square (47). Spawn by the Well's mouth, a sun carved in it, dark.
;      A Glassbearer paces in the city's shadow, his glass dull: a glass only
;      burns in the sun.                       [Glassbearer: introduce]
;   2. The hall (44-47). A sentinel, and Chapter II's lift up a floor.
;      Secret A off the room it reaches; secret C in the hall's floor.
;   3. The face, in shade (32-40). Out onto the balconies: torch 1, a grab,
;      R1, a grab.
;   4. The first light (31): the horizon. The mirror on the balcony throws
;      the sun at the sky. Strike it once: it turns east, into the sun
;      carved on door 1, which fills and opens.      [mirrors: introduce]
;   5. The stairwell (22-31). The open door lets the sun onto mirror A; A
;      throws it up to B; strike B east, into door 2. Torch 2. Secret B:
;      strike A east instead, into the sun-sealed doorway.
;                                                    [mirrors: develop]
;   6. The lift shaft (19-27), up to R5.
;   7. R5 (17-19). A Glassbearer on the balcony outside fires through the
;      window. Parry his bolt home. Torch 3.  [Glassbearer: develop]
;   8. R6 (9-15). No sun reaches in. The Glassbearer outside fires through
;      the doorway at anything in line, and mirror C stands in the line:
;      strike D east, stand behind C and let him shoot -- C throws his bolt
;      up to D, D into door 3. Kill him instead and mirror E, outside in the
;      sun, does the same work. R2 on the pillar three tiles out.
;                                   [mirrors + Glassbearer: twist]
;   9. The stair and the roof hatch. Torch 4 on the roof.
;  10. The summit (6). A Glassbearer in the last gold, a sentinel at the
;      parapet, and the great mirror hanging off its end, throwing the sun at
;      the sky. Strike it twice: east -- the beam runs the roof to the great
;      door's step -- then down. The beam falls the tower's whole height into
;      the Well's mouth; the mouth carries mark 9, and so does the great door,
;      which opens as the sun goes under.
;                       [mirrors + Glassbearer: master; SET PIECE]
;
; Secrets:
;   A. The room the lift reaches: its east wall is cracked. A relic and
;      NO KING / KEEPS IT.
;   B. The stairwell's east wall: a doorway with a sun carved in it (Q).
;      Turn mirror A east -- away from B -- and it opens. A relic.
;   C. The hall's floor: cracked, two wide. A relic.
;
; Relics: R1 (balcony E4), R2 (the pillar), and one in each secret: five.
; Par 3:00.
;
