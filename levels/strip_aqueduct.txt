; The Crumbling Aqueduct
;! time sunset
;
; A duel strip (scripts/duel/duel_strips.gd): its zones, west to east.
;
; An aqueduct's deck at sunset, high over the channel it carries, with its
; old slabs cracked through. A slab lights the first frame a foot finds it,
; shudders for thirty-six frames and drops into the water, taking the one
; beside it with it, and it does not come back: the strip a match ends on is
; not the strip it began on. The state of every slab is one byte in the
; match's snapshot and checksum, so both phones wear the same holes into it.
;
; WHAT IT DOES TO A DUEL. It punishes standing still. Nidhogg's fencing is
; done from a planted stance at the edge of reach, and here a planted stance
; on cracked stone is a stance with a fuse under it. Every fight leaves its
; mark, the holes it leaves are moats a clash can knock a fighter into, and
; the run home gets harder for whoever wins the rope late.
;
;   zone 3  the Channel Span    the start: three pairs of cracked slabs
;   zone 2  the Broken Arches   the long span, five pairs between four piers
;   zone 1  the Aqueduct Gate   two pairs before the arch
;
; Zones 4 and 5 are 2 and 1 reversed. No run of cracked stone is wider than
; two slabs, so the deck can be worn down to jumps but never cut.
