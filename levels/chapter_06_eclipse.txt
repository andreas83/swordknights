; The Eclipse
;
; The finale of The Last Light: the Vault of the kept sun, at the bottom of
; the Sun Well (docs/campaign.md, "The Eclipse -- the boss"). Its own level
; rather than the end of Chapter V's, so that its checkpoint is the
; antechamber and the King's won phases outlive a death.
;
;! time night
;
; The antechamber (columns 1-5, rows 4-5): the start, and torch 4 one step
; on. Its lip drops five tiles onto the west gallery -- no further, and too
; far to climb back: once you are down, you are in it.
;
; The Vault (columns 6-31): a floor 26 tiles wide, which a 440-unit screen
; holds whole and a 320-unit screen pans a little. A gallery slab five tiles
; long at each end, four tiles up: high enough to plunge from, low enough to
; grab back onto from the floor at its lip. A heart on each, taken once.
; Under the dome the kept sun hangs on its chains over the King's post (K
; over E, one column), the only light down here.
;
; The King's four phases, each a lesson from an earlier chapter:
;   1. The Shield (the Bastion): he walks the floor lip to lip; plunge his
;      crown from a gallery as he pauses under it.
;   2. The Burning (the Glassbearer): volleys of sunbolts from under the
;      sun; parry them back into his chest.
;   3. The Eclipse (the Caracal): the vault goes dark and he pounces out of
;      it; backstep the leap and cut him while he skids.
;   4. The finale (the parry): the whole kept sun as one beam; parry it back
;      into the disc.
; A death comes back to the antechamber, with every phase already won kept.
;
; Dawn's door is in the east wall, under the east gallery. It stays shut
; while he stands; when he falls the kept sun rises up the Well, and the
; door fills with it and opens.
;
; Legend: E the Eclipse, K the kept sun; the rest as ever.
;
