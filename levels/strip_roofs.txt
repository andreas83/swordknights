; The Rooftops
;! time dusk
;
; A duel strip (scripts/duel/duel_strips.gd): its zones, west to east.
;
; The city's roofs at dusk, the sun burning down behind the domes, with the
; alleys between the houses open to the dark. Every gap is two tiles, a
; running jump, except the widest, which has a plank laid over it; none has a
; bottom, so a fall is out of the world and the rope goes to the other one.
; Chimneys to vault, a taller house to hop onto, washing on the lines.
;
; WHAT IT DOES TO A DUEL. It puts an edge under every exchange. Blade on
; blade knocks both fighters back (DuelCombat's clash), and on a roof that
; knock is a step toward an alley; a parry at the lip is a kill without a
; touch. The rooftops have no clock and nothing that moves -- static and
; obvious, the one strip whose hazards are all of them always visible -- and
; they are the hardest to run home across.
;
;   zone 3  the Ridge           the start: two alleys and a chimney
;   zone 2  the Tiles           an alley, a taller house, and the plank
;   zone 1  the Last Roof       an alley and a chimney before the arch
;
; Zones 4 and 5 are 2 and 1 reversed.
