; The Channel Span
;
; Zone 3 of 5 of the Crumbling Aqueduct, where every match starts: symmetric
; about its own centre, one spawn mark, mirrored by the match.
;
; The deck runs whole from wall to wall at the start of a match. Three pairs
; of its slabs are cracked stone (`,`, the campaign's crumbling stone): each
; one lights the first frame a foot finds it, shudders for six tenths of a
; second, and drops into the channel, taking its neighbour a quarter of a
; second later -- and it stays down for the rest of the match. A runner is
; across a pair before it goes; a fencer who stands on one is not. So the
; middle of the strip starts as a floor and is worn, by the fight itself,
; into three moats two tiles wide, each one a jump and each one a place to be
; parried into. The piers between them never fall.
;
; Every gap the stone can leave is two tiles, a running jump, because no
; slab run is wider: the strip can be worn down but never cut.
