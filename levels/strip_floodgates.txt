; The Floodgates
;! time golden
;
; A duel strip (scripts/duel/duel_strips.gd): its zones, west to east.
;
; A cistern quarter at the golden hour, where the city lets its reservoirs
; down through the courts on a timer. Each sunken court is the short way and
; the open place to fight while it is dry; then its sluices gush, the water
; climbs two tiles to the walkways in two and a half seconds, stands for six,
; and drains. Twenty seconds a cycle, on the match's own frame, so it is the
; same tide on both phones without a byte sent for it (StripHazards).
;
; WHAT IT DOES TO A DUEL. It moves the fight. A Nidhogg duel is fought where
; the floor is, and here the floor comes and goes: a fighter who reads the
; sluices takes the low road and leaves the other on the stones, and one who
; is pushed back into a court as it fills hands the rope over without a
; blade touching them. The middle court and the gates flood together; the
; long cisterns between flood on the other half of the cycle, so there is
; always a low road open somewhere.
;
;   zone 3  the Sluice Court    the start: a sunken court under stepping stones
;   zone 2  the Long Cistern    two basins and an island, out of phase
;   zone 1  the Floodgate       a moat that comes and goes, before the arch
;
; Zones 4 and 5 are 2 and 1 reversed, on the same tide.
